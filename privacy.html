This site is an unofficial fan project for rating songs and albums. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any of the artists it covers. Artist and song names are used for identification only, and all trademarks belong to their owners.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password. The password is handled by our sign-in provider (Supabase); we do not see or store it in readable form.
  • Your content: the ratings, notes, tags, settings and any images you choose to upload (for example custom album covers), plus the achievements and game results the site records for you.
  • On your device: a copy of your data is kept in your browser's local storage so the site works quickly and offline.

We do not currently run advertising or analytics tools, and we do not sell your data.

Public profile (optional)

If you choose to publish a public profile, your display name, your short bio and the top albums and songs you tick are stored in a separate table and shown to anyone who has your link. Nothing is published unless you press Publish, and you can unpublish at any time in Settings. Unpublishing or deleting your account removes it.

How we use it

Only to run the site: to sign you in, save your ratings and show them back to you across your devices.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase hosts sign-in and the database that stores your content.
  • jsDelivr (a content delivery network) serves the sign-in library, so it can see your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Settings, Account, "Download all my data" (or the Backups section on the home screen).
  • Delete your data and account: Settings, Account, "Delete my account & data". This is permanent.
  • Unpublish your public profile: Settings on the home screen, Public profile, "Unpublish".
  • Reset parts of your data: the reset buttons on the home screen's settings.

You can also contact us at [your contact email] to ask about your data.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it or your account. Deleting your account removes your saved data from our database.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
